# Risk Disclosure

Status: starter public-beta draft, not legal-reviewed.

VCB-Alt provides heuristic screening output for user review. It is not investment advice and does not guarantee any result.

## Market Risk

Stocks can lose value quickly. High-volatility archetypes can move sharply, gap overnight, or become unsuitable for many users.

## Data Risk

Market-data providers may be delayed, incomplete, stale, unavailable, or wrong. Price/volume-only providers do not supply fundamentals, news, short interest, options, analyst revisions, or company-specific catalysts.

## Model Risk

Scores are rules-based research signals. They can be wrong, incomplete, overfit to historical behavior, or misinterpreted. A high score does not mean a position should be opened.

## User Responsibility

Users must verify data, review risks, and make their own decisions. VCB-Alt does not place trades.

## Launch Requirement

This disclosure must be reviewed by qualified counsel before broad public launch.
